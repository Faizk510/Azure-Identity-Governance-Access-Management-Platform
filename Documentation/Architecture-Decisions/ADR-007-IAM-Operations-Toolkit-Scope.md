# ADR-007 – IAM Operations Toolkit Scope

## Status

Accepted

## Decision

The IAM Operations Toolkit was designed to provide practical operational reporting and administrative automation focused on common Microsoft Entra administration scenarios.

## Design Rationale

- Operational value was prioritised over the number of scripts.
- Related capabilities were consolidated where appropriate to reduce duplication.
- Reporting and automation were intentionally separated to improve usability and maintainability.
- The toolkit focuses on repeatable administrative tasks commonly performed by identity administrators.

## Architectural Impact

The toolkit complements the governance platform by extending day-to-day operational capabilities without introducing unnecessary complexity or overlapping functionality.

## Implementation

This decision is reflected through the reporting and automation modules that support administration, governance validation, and operational reporting.
